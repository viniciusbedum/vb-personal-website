## Example projects stay as hidden models

`content/projects/projeto-exemplo-*/` are models of the case format. When the first real project is added, hide them (remove their entries from `PROJECTS` and `FEATURED_PROJECTS`) instead of deleting them, and duplicate one to write a new project. If the person follows or may later follow updates from this template repository, leaving the shipped files untouched avoids conflicts.
